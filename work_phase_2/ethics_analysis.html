Ethics Analysis

Newsletter Subscription Feature

Privacy violation and Informed consent

  • By using the Newsletter Subscription feature, data will be collected and may lead to potential data breaches. This is especially true when scammers obtain the information, which could cause significant damage, such as credit card details and phone numbers. This issue could lead to users being harassed by scammers, such as receiving fraudulent phone calls, as is happening today.

According to the principle, H. develops systems that are robust, secure, and user-friendly. Developers should organize functions to protect data by using password security to access personal information, allowing users to set their own passwords when they need to view or edit their data.

According to the principle,F. Identify and mitigate any risks to others associated with your work. Developer should collect only significant data such as Name and Email.

To manage the tensions, the potential conflict is that the client wants to obtain data from users, but this action may be unethical. To reduce this conflict, we should explain the ethics of being a developer, outline the potential consequences according to the code of conduct, and suggest collecting only the necessary data while eliminating unused information. Specifically, for credit card details, when they are required, users should be encouraged to provide them on a case-by-case basis.

Ownership and Property and Distrust and Financial Abuse (Welfare)

  • By using the Newsletter Subscription feature, it may lead to a violation of data ownership rights, such as using the data for marketing purposes. For example, gathering user information to create products based on the data users provide without consent permitted. This contributes in users feeling exploited and wont't trust this system, which may also extend to other systems.

According to the principle, A.Be honest, open and truthful in all interaction with employers, employees, clients, stakeholders, and the public.Developer must provide the clear statement of the consent form what the purpose of developers and clients need to use the user's data

According to the principle, D. Respect the privacy, confidentiality and integrity of any personal or proprietary information that comes into your possession, including data relating to individuals or other stakeholders. And B. Practice integrity. Be consistent in your views, words and actions. Declare and manage any conflicts of interest. Do not allow the undue influence of others or bias to prevent you complying with this Code.Developers must protect personal information and not violate others' private property for marketing purposes without consent. Advanced password security should be used to control access to the data.

To manage the tensions, we should advise the effects that this action may be unethical althought the client needs all informations from the users. we should advise that users will be given the option to voluntarily answer questions without being forced. Additionally, there should be a clear warning displayed to users about how their data will be used.
