Accountability

Sometimes, AI may provide incorrect answers or information. Therefore, AI developers should continuously monitor and update the data to ensure that users receive the maximum benefit. Alternatively, users could be allowed to update the information themselves, after which AI developers must review and publish the corrected data. Thus,"accountability" is the most important factor that developers should realise and also users should analyze and use the information with discretion.

Privacy, Unfairness and Bias

AI may exhibit bias or discrimination in the information it provides. It often offers responses, suggestions, or insights based on the most favorable perspectives, while potentially overlooking negative or critical aspects that might also be useful for users to consider before making decisions. For example, when creating a client's persona, AI might track user behavior across applications before or utilize aggregated data to generate responses. This situation may raise concerns about user privacy, as personal information may be collected, adapted, and distributed without explicit consent.

Ownership & Property

Based on the client's persona, code generation, and the five open-ended questions, it is evident that AI often does not provide sources for the information it presents. This raises concerns about potential intellectual property infringement. Without proper attribution, users may unknowingly rely on content that lacks verified references, which could lead to negative consequences when the information is reused or shared.
