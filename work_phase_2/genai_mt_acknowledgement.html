HOW AI WAS USED

  • For this Assessment, I used ChatGPT to assist with 5 client's persona including Act, question and interview. I began by using AI to generate ideas related to my work area which is the hobby community hub. While this provided a useful starting point, I noticed that the responses were to general and lacked of some Information I needed because AI gave the best and short answers to me. To address this, I used the prompts as "Act as " which is the example from the course given. This led more relevant responses. Furthermore, I provided more specific statement as I needed.

I used ChatGPT as the translator to correct my grammar and I found that sometimes AI tried to change my meaning. To solve with this, I used the constraints word to control my meaning that "Do not change my meaning and give more information"

I used ChatGPT to help me generate CSS code following the class's guidance by providing context with the purpose and be specific with the clear list what I am looking for.

HOW AI SUPPORT LEARNING

  • To ensure AI-supported my learning rather than replacing my effort, I compared to criteria needs from this course and identified gaps where additional critical engagement was needed. However, I founded that AI did not align with the subject's criteria needs. Thus, I restructed some content based on manual analysis.

AI gave the information and follow step by step but I tried to read carefully and keep the information to correct those they gave

ETHICAL CONSIDERATION

Accountability

  • AI may provide incorrect answers or information.

Bias

  • AI may exhibit bias or discrimination in the information it provides.

Privacy

  • AI might track user behavior across applications before or utilize aggregated data to generate responses. This may raise concerns about user privacy without explicit consent.

Ownership & Property

  • AI does not provide sources for the information it presents. This raises concerns about potential intellectual property infringement. This lead to negative consequences when the information is reused or shared.

AI developers should continuously monitor and update the data to ensure that users receive the maximum benefit.
