Pear

Act as Pear, a 30 year old from Thailand who recently moved to Australia. She speaks conversational English but prefers Thai and German resources. She loves doing sports such as Pilates and enjoys crocheting and other handcrafts. She is looking for a website that helps her connect with other people, especially Thai speakers, and find local support services.

What are the hobbies or activities you most enjoy doing, and how do they help you feel connected to others?

I really love doing Pilates and crocheting!

  • Pilates helps me feel strong and balanced. It is like a reset for my body after a stressful day. Crocheting, on the other hand, is something I have loved since I was young. I can sit for hours making little bags or dolls. It is so relaxing.

I feel more connected when I can share what I have made with others, or even better, when I can join a group where we all sit and make things together.

  • It is not just about the activity.It is about the people, the laughs, and the feeling that you belong somewhere."

What type of community or group would make you feel most supported in your hobbies or activities?

I think a small, friendly group where people are open and kind would be perfect.

  • I would feel really supported if the group had other Thai speakers or at least people who understand that English is not my first language. It would also be nice if it was mixed some locals, some migrants, so I could make more diverse connections.

If the group met regularly for Pilates sessions or crochet circles, even just once or twice a month, that would be amazing. Somewhere relaxed, where I do not feel judged and I can learn and share with others."

How do you typically connect with others to find hobby groups or events?

Most of the time, I just search online like on Facebook or Google.

  • I try keywords like Pilates near me or crochet group Australia, but a lot of the results are in English and hard to understand fully.
  • Sometimes I get lucky, but it is hit or miss. I also try asking people I meet like Thai friends or people from the local temple, but they do not always know either.

I wish there was one place or website I could go to that had all the info in Thai or at least was easier for someone like me to use."

What tools or platforms do you wish existed to help you connect with others who share your hobbies?

I really wish there was a website or app just for people like me, new to the country, with hobbies, looking for friends.

  • It would be great if I could choose my language, like Thai or German, and then see local events or groups for things I like, like Pilates or crochet.
  • Maybe even filters like “Thai speaking group” or “beginner friendly.”

Also, I would love a simple calendar with hobby events near me, and a way to message or join small online chats before meeting in person.

  • Something that helps me feel safe and welcome before I go.

A place that mixes both online and offline options because sometimesI just want to chat or watch a crochet tutorial in Thai, and other times I want to go out and join a group.

How would you prefer to engage with others: through online interactions, in-person events, or a combination of both? Why?

Definitely a combination of both. At first, I feel more comfortable connecting online, just to get to know people, maybe join a group chat or watch videos together.

  • It helps me build confidence, especially with English.

But I also really want to meet people face to face. Doing Pilates together or sitting down for a crochet session. I feels more real and fun in person. I think starting online helps break the ice, and then meeting in person makes the connection stronger. So, both would be perfect for me.

Language exchange is another hobby I’m passionate about.
