Client Needs Analysis

The client Brief

About Us

The Hobby Community Hub, we are a non-profit organisation dedicated to fostering social cohesion by empowering diverse communities to share stories, connect with others, and access resources that enhance their visibility and influence. Our mission is to strengthen societal bonds by creating platforms that celebrate and support the unique identities of communities across Australia.

We believe that hobbies are gateways to a sense of belonging and connection within a community. This platform is designed to be a welcoming, inclusive and accessibility, and accessible space where individuals of all backgrounds can explore new interests, promote unique activities, share learning experiences, collaborate, and build meaningful relationships with a flexible group without a commitment together. User's hobbies can be connected with the community and the most important thing is committed to accessibility that this platform is user-friendly and available to everyone.

Project Overview

The Hobby Community Hub is dynamic, welcoming highly inclusive platform designed to connect, inspire and empower individuals passionate about unique and less - known activities. The platform is user-friendly websites for a wide range users can navigate and participate with ease.This website represents and supports different communities and serve as a central hub where members can share and do the social connecting, create resources, build community and highlight events, and foster meaningful connections by helping the users to find hobby groups, workshops and social events tailored to passions when the users needs. This project consistents with the Connect Communities Initiative (CCI) mission by creating an accessible needs and spaces where those interested in can discover new interests, shared experiences, build meaningful connections and fostering a strong sense of belonging. Hence, this is designed to accommodate a wide range of needs, includes non-native speakers that they can feel comfortable and engaged when they are using the platform. The platform is also suitable for those with busy schedule, but prefer having an interaction for both online and in-person participation which is suit different lifestyles in each users. Above all, the Hobby Community Hub fosters a safe environment where the member can be feel comfortable and collaborate without fear of judgment.
