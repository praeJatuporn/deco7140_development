Reflective Design.

This is Ethical response to client Newsletter Subscribtion plugin for the handcrafts community.

There are 4 outlines including Introduction, Ethical reasoning, Recommendation and Client Prespective.

We should response to our client by Saying thank you that they interest in adding the newsletter subscription feature to the website and focus on their goals why they need this feature.

Example

"Thank you for your interest in adding a newsletter subscription feature to the website. We understand and respect your intention to improve communication and build stronger engagement with your community. We understand and respect your goal to improve communication and increase community engagement. This feature can help share updates about activities such as workshops, host an event, and local support services, while also fostering a stronger sense of connection and inclusion within the your community. The newsletter can be helpful for users who may not always have the time or ability to check for events or access community resources. It allows them to stay informed and involved in the community from wherever they are".

Users can feel intrusive or disrespectful by asking for personal information such as name and email address without clearly explaining how that data will be used. If the informations is used for marketing or shared with others without permission, honesty, trustworthiness and respect may be lost.

Ethical principles

ACS Code A : Honesty

Be honest, open and truthful in all interaction with employers, employees, clients, stakeholders, and the public.Developer must provide the clear statement of the consent form what the purpose of developers and clients need to use the user's data

ACS Code D: Respect for Privacy

Respect the privacy, confidentiality and integrity of any personal or proprietary information that comes into your possession, including data relating to individuals or other stakeholders.

Reason

According to the client, there are users who are Thai and Chinese, may have limited English proficiency. However, these are not the only groups to consider. Future users from various cultural backgrounds may also struggle to understand the purpose of data collection.

Recommendation

Using clear and simple language to explain data usage

Place a short, clear statement near the form that explains why each piece of information is being collected and how it will be used.

>

Users who have visual impairments, motor disabilities, or limited technology access, may struggle to use the newsletter subscription form if it is not designed with accessibility including missing keyboard navigation, unclear labels, or a lack of focus indicators for users who do not use a mouse. This contribute to users will not receive updates, services and opportunity that could benefit them.

Ethical principles

ACS Code C : Fairness

Be fair and not discriminate unfairly against people in interpersonal interactions or in the design and function of systems. Developers should ensure that all users, regardless of ability, have equal access to content and features.

ACS Code D: Respect for Privacy and Integrity

Respect the integrity and usability of systems for everyone, including users who rely on assistive technologies. Systems should be built with inclusive access in mind to uphold digital dignity.

Reason

The website supports a wide range of users. If the form cannot be accessed via keyboard or lacks a visible focus indicator, these users may be excluded without intention. Thus, it is important that everyone should deserve equal access to information and participation.

Recommendation

From WCAG 1.3.1 (Info and Relationships): Using semantic html "field" and "legend" to group the form structure.

From WCAG 2.4.7 (Focus Visible): use the border to ensure that the filled box are clearly visible for keyboard users.

From WCAG 3.3.2 (Labels or Instructions): use labels or hints to let the users know.

>

Users may feel uncomfortable when they are asked to answer their personal or sensitive information such as phone number, gender, credit card number, etc. This leads to a sense of intrusion and reducing user trust, participation and autonomy if the form does not explain the reasons.

Ethical principles

ACS Code C : Fairness

Be fair and not discriminate unfairly against people in interpersonal interactions or in the design and function of systems. Developers should ensure that all users, regardless of ability, have equal access to content and features.

ACS Code B: Practice Integrity

Be consistent and transparent in your actions. Do not request data without a justifiable reason. Clarify intentions to prevent mistrust or misuse of user information.

Reason

Users may feel reluctant to share personal information if they are unsure how it will be used, especially people who comes from diverse background such as LGBTQ. They may avoid completing the form and feel unsafe when using website or engaging with community.

Recommendation

Limit data collection

Avoid asking for personal information such as gender or phone number when it is not necessary for the community purpose. Alternatively, if such fields are included, clearly label them as "optional" so users understand that they can choose whether or not to provide that information without any negative consequences.

>

Although the newsletter subscription feature may seem lightweight but collecting unnecessary information can contribute to a higher workload on the server. The result is leading to long-term environmental costs. This can increase energy consumption.

Ethical principles

ACS Code C: Promote Environmental Sustainability

Seek to enhance, in the professional choices you make, the environmental sustainability of ICT systems and the overall quality of life of those affected by them. Developers have a responsibility to avoid wasteful system design and reduce unnecessary digital load wherever possible.

Reason

Our website aims to support the broader community, and that includes caring about environmental sustainability. By limiting the amount of data we store, we can reduce energy use and help create a system that is more efficient, ethical, and environmentally friendly. Even though users might not notice the environmental impact right away, it can become a serious issue over time.

Recommendation

Design the form to collect only essential information following accessibility standards.

The client’s goal is to build strong relationships with the local community through communication, news, and activities shared on the website. The ethical recommendations including clearly explaining data usage, making the form accessible, and avoiding unnecessary data collection, help build trust, make users feel safe, and encourage participation. These actions not only support short-term goals like increasing newsletter sign-ups or incresing members but also help the organisation be seen as honest, inclusive, and socially responsible. They help show that the organisation respects user rights, communicates clearly, and supports long-term engagement with the community.
