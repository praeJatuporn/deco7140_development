Site Map.

This is the website created by the Card Sort exercise

In this exercise, I created a sitemap based on the card sort results. The goal is to create a user-friendly navigation structure.

This accordion shows the website structure. Expand each section to view pages and sub-pages, with notes on user or client needs.

The About helps users understand the mission of the website and how it connects people with shared interests. It helps users decide if the platform meets their needs. The design is simple, making it easy to scan and understand the purpose. Users can connect with groups based on interests including crochet, knitting and embroidery.

A section provides a clear explanation of the website's purpose and values, helping users understand how this platform can meet their needs. For new users, this section allows them to connect with others who share their love for handcrafts such as knitting, embroidery, and crochet. It makes it easier for them to read and decide if they want to be part of the community. The design is straightforward, with clear headings and easy to scan content. It also includes high contrast text and a clear layout to support users with visual impairments.

The FAQ section addresses the common questions and concerns users may have, providing clear and concise answers to help them understand the website’s features, activities, and how to connect with others. To support users, it offers easy to scan text with clearly distinguished headings for each question, ensuring they can quickly find the information they need to use the site effectively and join relevant activities.

The simple contact template for user who may have questions, feedback, or need support.This is designed to be fully accessible with a clear instruction and labels for each field in the contact form.The call to action button is prominently displayed to guide users through the process of submitting their message.

The central hub for each user to manage their personal information, interests, and activity within the community. This will be linked to the group profile. Supports the client goal of a sense of belonging. It provides a clear layout with easy-to-use forms for updating personal details, interests, and activities.

This allows users to manage and update their personal information such as name, contact details, and preferences. Users can connect with like minded individuals by creating the specific group. The design is user-friendly layout with the simple input and edit information including the clear form fields with labeled.

A showcase of inspiring handcrafts through the posting of photos or videos. It supports users' unique activities and their work while fostering meaningful connections with others through interactions, such as liking posts with the button.
