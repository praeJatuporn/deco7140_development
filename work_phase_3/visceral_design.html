Visceral Design.

The focus of this section of the Style Guide is ensuring the user has a good first impression of the site.


Page Layout

Colour Scheme

This ideal colour palette was chosen to reflect a warm, yarn-like, welcoming, and creative atmosphere that suits the handcraft community. It served as a conceptual foundation and was carefully adapted and applied to the overall webpage design to ensure emotional engagement, visual coherence, and accessibility.

Yellow – Page background (warm & calming)

Green – Buttons and call-to-action

White – Content area (clean & readable)

Black – Text (high contrast for accessibility)

Typography

The typography of this website is designed for readability, clarity, and inclusiveness. Font choices help users, especially non-native English speakers, easily scan and understand the content.

"Poppins" font for all headings and body text. This font was chosen for its modern, clean appearance and strong readability.

A sans-serif, it supports clarity across different screen sizes and works well for both native and non-native English speakers.

Typography Rules WCAG


1. Using no more than 2-3 font types Using font "Poppins" is a modern sans-serif that is very readable. To ensures visual consistency and readability.

2. Create a visual hierarchy Applying different sizes clearly such as h1: 3rem(48px), h2: 1.5 rem(24px), p: 1 rem(16px) etc.

3. Use scale: rem (1.4.4 Resize Text) Applying rem scale with the font size such as h1: 3rem, h2: 1.5 rem), p: 1 rem etc. instead of fixed px for flexible layouts and responsive.

4. Clear font-weight usage (1.3.1 Info and Relationships) Applying font-weight such as bold. To help convey structure without relying on color alone

5.Line height (Technique C29) Applying line-height 1.5 to improves reading comfort.

6. High color contrast (1.4.3 Contrast Minimum) Applying the color between text and background which the text is black and background is white.
