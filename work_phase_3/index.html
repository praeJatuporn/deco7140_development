🧵Handcrafts Club..

The Handicrafts club is the community connection initiative. This website outlines the structure, design, and features that support members in discovering and connecting through shared hobbies.


Purpose of the Website

This website will help connect people with an interest in Handicrafts.


Website Features

The website will allow users to share their works, learn new techniques, and participate in online activities or meetings.


Accessibility and Design

The website is easily accessible for all users, particularly those with special needs or disabilities.


Community Building

The platform will enable users to connect and network with other members of the Handicrafts club.
